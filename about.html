About Us

be complicated

TrackHive is a free, no-account-required package tracker that works across all four major US carriers — USPS, FedEx, UPS, and DHL — in one place.

Why we built TrackHive

Most people receive packages from multiple carriers every week. Tracking them means visiting four different websites, creating accounts, and navigating interfaces that were designed for logistics professionals — not everyday shoppers.

TrackHive fixes that. Paste in a tracking number — any number, from any carrier — and get to the live tracking page in one click. No account. No signup. No friction.

We also built bulk tracking for people managing multiple shipments at once: small business owners, resellers, office managers, and anyone else who receives more packages than they can reasonably track one at a time.

How TrackHive works

Simple by design. TrackHive doesn't reinvent tracking — it removes the friction from it.

Enter a tracking number

Paste any tracking number into the search box. TrackHive accepts numbers from USPS, FedEx, UPS, and DHL — you don't need to know which carrier it belongs to.

Carrier auto-detected

Each carrier uses a distinct number format. TrackHive recognizes the pattern instantly — UPS numbers start with 1Z, USPS numbers begin with 9, and so on — and identifies the right carrier automatically.

Live tracking opens instantly

TrackHive opens the official carrier's tracking page with your number pre-filled. You see live status directly from the source — the carrier's own systems — with no delays or third-party data.

What TrackHive is — and isn't

We want to be upfront about how the site works.

TrackHive is

  • ✓ A free tool that identifies your carrier and opens the official tracking page
  • ✓ An independent, third-party service not affiliated with any carrier
  • ✓ A bulk tracker for managing multiple shipments at once
  • ✓ Privacy-respecting — your tracking numbers never leave your browser

TrackHive is not

  • ✕ An official USPS, FedEx, UPS, or DHL service or website
  • ✕ A real-time tracking database — all live data comes from the carrier directly
  • ✕ A service that stores or logs your tracking numbers on a server
  • ✕ A subscription or paid service of any kind
